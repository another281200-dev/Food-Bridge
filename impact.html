How We Calculate Our Impact Metrics

1. Food Waste Diversion & Weight Verification

Every food listing requires a verified weight recorded on commercial kitchen scales or container unit counts (such as standard bread trays or produce bushel crates). These weights are confirmed by recipient shelter staff during digital intake sign-off.

2. Climate & Carbon Emissions Modeling

Our greenhouse gas reductions use the United States EPA Waste Reduction Model (WARM) and IPCC guidelines. Every 1 lb of mixed edible food diverted from an anaerobic landfill environment prevents approximately 2.5 lbs of atmospheric carbon dioxide equivalent (CO₂e).
