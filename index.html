How FoodBridge Operates in Lucknow

From wedding banquets and sabzi mandis to neighborhood community kitchens.

1

Banquets & Mandis List Food

Wedding halls in Hazratganj, bakeries, and Dubagga mandi traders post surplus rotis, dal, rice, or fresh vegetables with available pickup windows.

2

Langars & Shelters Claim Instantly

Gurdwaras, temple annakshetras, and night shelters receive immediate alerts on WhatsApp and reserve the exact quantity they can serve.

3

E-Rickshaws & Volunteers Pickup

Dedicated electric rickshaws and community volunteers transport food in insulated containers across Lucknow within 45 minutes.
